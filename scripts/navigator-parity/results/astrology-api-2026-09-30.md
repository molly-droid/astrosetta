### Q25 (D) — 3.4s

> Explain what "retrograde" physically means and why astrologers care.

Physically, a planet is in **retrograde** when it appears to be moving backward through the zodiac from our perspective on Earth. It’s an optical illusion caused by Earth moving faster in its orbit and "lapping" a slower-moving outer planet, or being lapped by a faster inner one. Think of it like passing a slower car on the highway—for a moment, that car seems to move backward relative to you.

Astrologers care because we view this as a shift in energy:

- **Internalization:** The planet’s outward expression feels "paused" or redirected inward. It’s less about initiating new action and more about review, reflection, and re-doing.
- **Complexity:** When a planet appears to stall, the areas of life it governs (e.g., communication for Mercury, relationships for Venus) often require extra patience, attention to detail, and a willingness to revisit past issues.
- **Correction:** Retrogrades are not "bad"; they are essential cosmic "correction cycles" that force us to slow down and ensure our foundations are solid before we move forward again.
