A THANK-YOU FOR BETA TESTING

A card is required at checkout. Choose monthly or yearly billing. Your first payment is January 1, 2027 at $7.77/month or $77/year, unless you cancel beforehand in Manage billing.

After starting your trial on the website, the same Premium access works in the Android app and, once available, the iPhone app. You can also keep using Free. Premium includes all Core features.

iPHONE USERS

We're still in review with the Apple App Store, and we'll let you know the moment it's ready to download. Until then, you can keep using the browser version and add it to your home screen for an app-like experience:

1. Open astrosetta.com in Safari
2. Tap Share, the square with an upward arrow. Open the page menu or “More” first if needed.
3. Choose “Add to Home Screen”, turn on “Open as Web App” if shown, then tap “Add”.

View the Home Screen instructions on the website
